---
ref: Isaiah 54:10
title: God's Love Never Fails
---
## Truth
God's loving devotion and peace will never leave us, because Jesus made a way for us to be close to God.

- Mountains and hills can move, but God's love won't.
- God promised to always be with His people.
- His promise of peace will never be broken.

## Do it today
Today, remember that God's love for you is super strong and will never change. You can trust Him with everything.

## Pray
God, thank You for Your amazing love that never gives up on us and for the peace Jesus gives. Amen.
