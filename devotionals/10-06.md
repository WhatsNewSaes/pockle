---
ref: Psalm 50:15
title: God Rescues You
---
## Truth
God loves to help us when we are in trouble, and He is always strong enough to do it.

- God is always there for you.
- He wants to help you when things are hard.
- He is powerful enough to deliver you.

## Do it today
When you have a problem today, big or small, talk to God about it. Ask Him to help you.

## Pray
God, thank You for always being there to rescue me when I am in trouble. I love You.
