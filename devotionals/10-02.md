---
ref: Psalm 37:5
title: Commit and Trust God
---
## Truth
God loves us and wants us to trust Him with everything, knowing He will always help us.

- Give your plans and worries to God.
- Believe God will keep His promises.
- God will work things out for your good.

## Do it today
Today, tell God about something you are worried about or a dream you have, and ask Him to help.

## Pray
God, thank You for loving me and promising to help me when I trust in You with all my heart.
