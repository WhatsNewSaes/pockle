---
ref: Luke 18:27
title: God Makes It Possible
---
## Truth
God can do anything, and He makes a way for us to be with Him through Jesus.

- Jesus shows us that nothing is too hard for God.
- Even when we can't do something, God can.
- God helps us when we trust in Him.

## Do it today
When you feel something is too hard, remember that God is with you and can help you. Ask Him to show you His way.

## Pray
God, thank You that You are powerful and can do all things. Help me to trust You today.
