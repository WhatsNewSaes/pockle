---
ref: Ephesians 4:2
title: Love and Patience
---
## Truth
God helps us to be kind and patient with others because He first loved us with perfect patience.

- Jesus shows us how to be truly humble.
- He was gentle even when people were mean.
- He patiently loves us every single day.

## Do it today
Today, try to be extra patient with someone in your family or at school. Remember how Jesus is patient with you.

## Pray
God, thank You for loving me with so much patience. Help me to show that same love to others today.
