---
ref: Psalm 103:1
title: Praise the Lord
---
## Truth
God is so good to us, giving us good things and forgiving our sins.

- Praise God with all your heart.
- Remember all the good things God does.
- God forgives us and makes us new.

## Do it today
Today, think of one good thing God has given you and thank Him for it.

## Pray
God, thank You for loving me and forgiving my sins through Jesus. Help me to praise You today.
