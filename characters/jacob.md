Jacob was a twin who loved God! He had an amazing dream about a ladder to heaven with angels. God promised to be with him and bless his family, showing us that God always keeps His promises, just like He sent Jesus to us.

- Had a special dream about a ladder to heaven
- God changed his name to Israel
- Father of 12 sons who became 12 tribes
