David was a shepherd boy who trusted God more than he feared a giant. God chose him to be king of Israel, and he wrote many of the Psalms. Jesus was born from David's family, just as God promised.

- Beat Goliath with a sling, a stone, and faith in God
- Wrote songs to God, like Psalm 23
- Made big mistakes, but always came back to God
