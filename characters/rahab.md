Rahab was a brave woman in Jericho. She helped God's spies escape, even when it was dangerous! Because she trusted God, He saved her and her family when Jericho fell. God is full of grace and can save anyone who believes in Him.

- Lived in Jericho
- Helped Israelite spies
- Saved by a scarlet cord
