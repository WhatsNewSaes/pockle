Cornelius was a good man who loved God, even though he wasn't Jewish. God sent an angel to tell him to find Peter. When Peter shared about Jesus, Cornelius and his whole family believed and received the Holy Spirit! This showed that God's love and salvation are for everyone.

- Roman army captain
- Prayed and gave to the poor
- First Gentile believer
