Miriam was Moses' older sister. She was very brave and smart! When baby Moses was in a basket in the river, Miriam watched over him. She helped her mom get to nurse baby Moses, even though Pharaoh's daughter found him. God used her to protect Moses!

- Moses' big sister
- Brave and smart
- Helped save baby Moses
