Rebekah was a kind and helpful woman who lived long ago. God chose her to marry Isaac, Abraham's son, because she was so good to strangers and their animals. Her story shows us how God has a special plan for each of us, and how being kind can lead to wonderful things!

- Carried water for a stranger and his camels.
- Brave to leave home and marry Isaac.
- God had a special plan for her life.
