Timothy was a young helper to Paul, a great missionary! He learned about Jesus from his mom and grandma. Timothy was brave and helped tell many people about God's amazing love and grace.

- Learned about God from his family.
- Traveled with Paul on his journeys.
- Helped share the good news of Jesus.
