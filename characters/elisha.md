Elisha was a special prophet who followed God's friend Elijah. When Elijah went to heaven, Elisha got double his power! God used Elisha to do amazing miracles, like bringing a boy back to life. This shows us how God cares for us and how Jesus has power over death too!

- Got double power from Elijah
- Made a widow's oil never run out
- Brought a boy back to life
