Balaam was a prophet who was asked to curse God's people, but God made him bless them instead! Even when people try to do bad things, God can change their plans to do good. He always protects His children, just like Jesus protects us.

- Prophet hired to curse Israel
- His donkey talked to him!
- God made him bless Israel
