Elijah was a brave prophet who told people to follow God, not fake gods. God showed everyone that He is the only true God by sending fire from heaven when Elijah prayed. This shows how God is powerful and always keeps His promises.

- Fed by ravens during a drought
- God sent fire from heaven for his sacrifice
- Never died, taken to heaven in a whirlwind
