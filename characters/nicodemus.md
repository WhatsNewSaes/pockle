Nicodemus was a very important teacher who came to Jesus at night to learn more. Jesus taught him about being born again, which means having a new heart from God. This story shows how Jesus welcomes everyone, even important people, to understand His love and grace.

- Important teacher
- Came to Jesus at night
- Learned about being "born again"
