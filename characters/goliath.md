Goliath was a giant warrior from the Philistines, over nine feet tall. For forty days he dared Israel's army to fight him, and everyone was afraid - until a boy named David stepped up.

- Wore heavy armor and carried a huge spear
- Laughed at David for being small
- Shows that God's power is bigger than any giant
