Goliath was a huge, strong warrior who scared everyone! He challenged God's people, but a brave young shepherd named David trusted God and won a great victory. This shows that with God, even the smallest can overcome the biggest challenges.

- A giant warrior from the Philistines.
- He was over 9 feet tall!
- Defeated by young David and a slingshot.
