Absalom was King David's handsome son. He tried to take his father's kingdom, which made David very sad. Absalom's story reminds us that being disobedient can lead to sad endings, but God always offers forgiveness and new beginnings.

- Son of King David
- Tried to take his father's throne
- Caught in a tree by his hair
