Caleb was a brave leader who trusted God completely! When others were scared to enter the Promised Land, Caleb said, "We can do it with God's help!" God loved Caleb's faith and gave him a special mountain as his own.

- One of the 12 spies sent to Canaan
- Trusted God when others were afraid
- Received a mountain as his reward
