Matthew was a tax collector, collecting money for the Roman government. Most people didn't like tax collectors, but Jesus saw Matthew's heart. Jesus called Matthew to follow Him, and Matthew left everything to become Jesus' friend and helper. This shows how Jesus loves everyone, no matter what job they do!

- Was a tax collector
- Jesus called him to follow
- Became one of Jesus' 12 special friends
