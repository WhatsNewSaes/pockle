Andrew was one of Jesus' first friends and a disciple! He was always bringing people to Jesus, like his brother Peter and a boy with some food. God used Andrew to help Jesus feed thousands of people with just a little bit of bread and fish. Andrew shows us how even small acts of kindness can do amazing things with God's help!

- One of Jesus' first friends
- Brother of Peter
- Helped feed 5000 people
