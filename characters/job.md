Job was a good man who loved God. He lost everything he had, even his children, but he never stopped trusting God. God gave him back even more than he had before. This shows us that God is always good and faithful, and He blesses those who love Him, just like Jesus blesses us.

- Lived in the land of Uz
- Lost everything but kept faith
- God restored all he lost and more
