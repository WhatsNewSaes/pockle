Esther was a brave queen who saved her people! An evil man wanted to hurt all the Jews, but Esther bravely spoke to the king, even though it was very risky. God used her courage to protect many lives, showing us His great love and grace for His people. Her story reminds us that God always has a plan.

- Became queen of Persia
- Bravely spoke to the king
- Saved her people from danger
