Mary was a young woman chosen by God to be the mother of Jesus, God's own Son! She humbly said "yes" to God's special plan. It shows how God picks ordinary people for amazing things and always keeps His promises.

- Chosen to be Jesus' mother
- Visited by the angel Gabriel
- Trusted God's special plan
