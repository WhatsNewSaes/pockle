Jonah was a prophet who didn't want to tell the city of Nineveh about God. He tried to run away, but God sent a big fish to swallow him! After three days, the fish spit Jonah out, and he finally obeyed God. This story reminds us that God gives second chances, just like Jesus rose after three days.

- Swallowed by a big fish
- Preached to Nineveh
- Learned to obey God
