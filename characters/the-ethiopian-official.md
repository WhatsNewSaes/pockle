This important official from Ethiopia was reading the Bible but didn't understand it. God sent Philip to explain. When he learned about Jesus, he wanted to be baptized right away! This shows how God wants everyone to know about Him.

- From Ethiopia, a faraway land
- Was reading Isaiah in his chariot
- Philip explained Jesus to him
