Barnabas was a kind and encouraging friend to Paul. He helped Paul meet other Christians and travel to new places to tell everyone about Jesus. Barnabas showed God's grace by always giving people a second chance.

- His name means "Son of Encouragement"
- Helped Paul meet other Christians
- Traveled with Paul to share Jesus
