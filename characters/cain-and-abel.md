Cain and Abel were brothers, the first children ever born! Cain grew plants and Abel took care of sheep. They both brought gifts to God, but God liked Abel's gift best because it showed his heart. This made Cain very, very sad and angry. He later hurt his brother, which was a terrible sin.

- First brothers ever born
- Cain farmed, Abel herded sheep
- God cares about our hearts
