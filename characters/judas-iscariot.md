Judas was one of Jesus's twelve special friends. But he made a very sad choice to betray Jesus to people who wanted to hurt Him. Judas showed them who Jesus was with a kiss, which led to Jesus's arrest.

- One of Jesus's 12 disciples
- Betrayed Jesus with a kiss
- Felt deep sadness afterwards
