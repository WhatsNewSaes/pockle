Hezekiah was a good king who loved God. When a powerful enemy army came to attack, Hezekiah prayed to God for help. God heard his prayer and sent an angel to save his people! This shows how God always listens to us when we trust Him, just like Jesus taught us.

- A good king who trusted God.
- Prayed when a big army came.
- God sent an angel to save his people.
