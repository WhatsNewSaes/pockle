Joseph was a kind carpenter chosen by God to be Jesus's earthly father. He listened to angels in dreams and always protected his special family. Joseph shows us how God takes care of us, even when things are confusing.

- Jesus's earthly dad
- Listened to angels in dreams
- Protected Mary and Jesus
