Samuel was a special prophet who heard God's voice even as a boy. God chose him to pick new leaders for His people, like when he anointed young David to be king. Samuel showed that God sees our hearts, not just what's on the outside, and chooses people for His big plans, just like He chose Jesus to be our forever King.

- Heard God's voice when he was young
- Anointed David to be king
- A prophet who spoke for God
