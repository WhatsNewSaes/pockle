Moses was a special leader chosen by God to free His people, the Israelites, from slavery in Egypt. God used Moses to do amazing things, like parting the Red Sea! God gave Moses the Ten Commandments to help His people live good lives. Moses' story helps us see how God always rescues His people, just like Jesus rescues us from our sins.

- Rescued from a basket as a baby
- Led God's people out of Egypt
- Received the Ten Commandments
