Aaron was Moses' older brother and helped him lead God's people out of Egypt. He spoke for Moses because Moses was shy. Later, Aaron made a golden calf, which made God sad. But God forgave him and still chose him to be the first high priest, showing us God's amazing grace!

- Moses' older brother
- Helped lead God's people
- God's first high priest
