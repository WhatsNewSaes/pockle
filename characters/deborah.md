Deborah was a very smart and brave leader who helped God's people, Israel. She was a judge, which meant she helped people make good choices, and a prophetess, which meant she shared God's messages. God used her to help Israel win a big battle! Her story shows us that God can use anyone, even a woman, to do amazing things for Him and to show His power.

- A wise leader and prophetess
- Helped Israel win a big battle
- Showed God uses everyone
