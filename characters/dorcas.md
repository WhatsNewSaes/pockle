Dorcas was a kind lady who loved to help others, especially by sewing clothes for people who needed them. When she died, everyone was so sad! But God used Peter to bring her back to life, showing His amazing power and love for those who serve Him.

- Lived in Joppa
- Made clothes for the poor
- Raised from the dead by Peter
