James was one of Jesus' special friends, called to follow Him while fishing with his brother John and their dad. He saw Jesus do amazing things, like shining super bright on a mountain! James was a brave leader who loved Jesus very much, showing us how to follow God's plan.

- Brother of John
- One of Jesus' first disciples
- Saw Jesus' Transfiguration
