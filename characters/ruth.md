Ruth was a kind and loyal woman who stayed with her mother-in-law, Naomi, even when times were very hard. She worked hard in the fields to find food for them both. God blessed her faithfulness, and her family tree even led to King David and Jesus!

- Loyal to her mother-in-law, Naomi
- Worked hard in the fields
- Her family led to Jesus
