Jeremiah was a special prophet chosen by God when he was very young. He had to tell people difficult messages, even when they didn't want to listen. God was always with Jeremiah, just like He is with us, showing His amazing grace.

- God chose him as a young prophet.
- He shared God's message, even when hard.
- God promised to be with him always.
