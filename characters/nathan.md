Nathan was a brave prophet who worked for God. He told King David a special story to help David see that he had done something wrong. Nathan helped David understand that God wants us to be fair and kind to everyone, just like Jesus teaches us to be.

- God's prophet and messenger
- Bravely told King David the truth
- Helped David ask God for forgiveness
