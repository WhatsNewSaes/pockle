Mary Magdalene was a special friend of Jesus. On the first Easter morning, she was the first to see that Jesus' tomb was empty! Then, Jesus appeared to her, and she was the first to tell everyone that Jesus was alive. This shows how much Jesus loved her and chose her to share the best news ever!

- First to see Jesus' empty tomb
- First to see Jesus after He rose
- First to tell others Jesus is alive!
