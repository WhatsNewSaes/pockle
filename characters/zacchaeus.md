Zacchaeus was a short man who really wanted to see Jesus! He climbed a tree so he could see over the crowd. Jesus saw him and came to his house, showing that Jesus loves everyone and wants to be with them.

- He was a tax collector.
- He climbed a sycamore tree.
- Jesus came to his house.
