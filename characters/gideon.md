Gideon was a brave leader God chose to save His people. Even though Gideon felt small, God helped him win a big battle with only 300 men! This shows that God can use anyone, no matter how weak they feel, to do amazing things, just like Jesus helps us.

- God chose Gideon to save His people.
- Won a big battle with only 300 men.
- God uses the weak to do great things.
