Solomon was a wise king who asked God for wisdom to lead his people well. God gave him great understanding, and he became famous for his fair judgments. His wisdom reminds us that Jesus is the wisest king of all.

- Asked God for wisdom, not riches
- Judged fairly between two mothers
- Wrote many wise sayings (Proverbs)
