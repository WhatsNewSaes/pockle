Nehemiah was sad because Jerusalem's walls were broken down. He asked God for help and then bravely asked the king if he could go fix them! Even when bad guys tried to stop him, Nehemiah trusted God and led the people to rebuild the walls. God helped them finish the big job!

- Cupbearer to the king
- Inspected Jerusalem's broken walls
- Led people to rebuild the walls
