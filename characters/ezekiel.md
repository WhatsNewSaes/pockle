Ezekiel was a special messenger from God. God showed him a valley full of dry bones and told him to speak to them! When Ezekiel spoke, the bones came back to life, showing us that God can make anything new again, even our hearts.

- God's special messenger
- Saw a valley of dry bones
- Bones came back to life!
