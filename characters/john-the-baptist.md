John the Baptist was a special messenger sent by God to tell everyone that Jesus was coming! He wore camel hair clothes and ate locusts and wild honey. He taught people to turn away from bad things and get ready for God's love. John helped people understand that Jesus is the Savior God promised.

- God's special messenger
- Told people Jesus was coming
- Taught about turning to God
