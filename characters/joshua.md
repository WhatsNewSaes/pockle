Joshua was a brave leader who took over after Moses. God helped Joshua and his people cross a big river and even made the walls of a strong city fall down just by shouting! God was with Joshua, just like Jesus is always with us.

- Took over leading God's people
- Led people across the Jordan River
- Walls of Jericho fell down
