Isaac was Abraham's special son, a gift from God! One day, God asked Abraham to sacrifice Isaac. But God stopped him, showing He provides a way, just like Jesus was the ultimate sacrifice for us. Isaac later had a family and continued to trust God, even when things were tough.

- Son of Abraham and Sarah
- God saved him from being sacrificed
- He dug wells and trusted God
