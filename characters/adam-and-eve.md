Adam and Eve were the very first people God made! They lived in a beautiful garden with God. But they chose to disobey God, which brought sadness into the world. God still loved them and promised to send a Rescuer, Jesus, to make things right again.

- First people God created
- Lived in a perfect garden
- Disobeyed God, but God still loved them
