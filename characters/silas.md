Silas was a good friend of Paul, helping him share God's good news. Even when they were beaten and put in jail for telling people about Jesus, they sang songs to God! God heard them and set them free, showing His amazing power and love.

- Traveled with Paul
- Sang praises in jail
- God set him free from prison
