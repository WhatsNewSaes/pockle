Bartimaeus was a blind man who sat by the road and begged. When he heard Jesus was nearby, he called out for help! Jesus heard him, and because Bartimaeus had faith, Jesus healed him so he could see again. This shows how Jesus cares for everyone.

- Blind man
- Sat by the road
- Jesus healed him
