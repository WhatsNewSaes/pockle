Thomas was one of Jesus' special friends. When Jesus came back to life, Thomas wasn't there at first and found it hard to believe. But Jesus lovingly showed Thomas his wounds, and then Thomas truly believed! This shows us that Jesus cares for everyone and helps us believe in Him.

- One of Jesus' 12 special friends
- Doubted Jesus was alive again
- Believed after seeing Jesus' wounds
