Lydia was a smart businesswoman who sold fancy purple cloth. She loved God and listened carefully when Paul talked about Jesus. Her heart opened up, and she and her whole family believed in Jesus and were baptized! This shows how God touches hearts with His grace.

- Sold purple cloth
- First Christian in Europe
- Opened her home to Paul
