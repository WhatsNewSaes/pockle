Samson was a super strong leader chosen by God to save his people from their enemies. Even when he made big mistakes and lost his strength, God still used him. His story reminds us that God can use anyone, and He always keeps His promises, just like Jesus saves us even when we mess up.

- God gave him amazing strength
- His long hair was a special promise to God
- He brought down a temple in the end
