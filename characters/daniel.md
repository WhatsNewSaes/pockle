Daniel was a young man who loved God very much. Even when bad men tried to stop him, Daniel kept praying to God every day. God protected Daniel when he was thrown into a den of hungry lions! This shows us that God always takes care of those who trust in Him, just like Jesus takes care of us.

- Prayed to God 3 times a day
- Thrown into a lions' den
- God saved him from the lions
