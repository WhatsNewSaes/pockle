Mary loved to listen to Jesus. She sat at His feet, learning from Him. Once, she poured expensive perfume on Jesus' feet and wiped them with her hair! This showed how much she loved and honored Jesus, preparing Him for a sad time ahead.

- Sister of Martha and Lazarus
- Loved listening to Jesus
- Anointed Jesus' feet with perfume
