Martha was a kind woman who loved Jesus and welcomed Him into her home. One time, she was very busy serving while her sister Mary sat and listened to Jesus. Martha learned that listening to Jesus is the most important thing. Later, when her brother Lazarus died, Martha believed Jesus could do anything, even raise him from the dead! This shows us Jesus is the King over death.

- Sister of Mary and Lazarus
- Loved to serve Jesus
- Believed Jesus is the Resurrection
